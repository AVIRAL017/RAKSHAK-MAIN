Frequently Asked Questions

SafeRoute Navigator uses a sophisticated algorithm that considers multiple factors including crime statistics, accident reports, lighting conditions, traffic patterns, and user feedback. The safety score is calculated on a scale of 0-10, with 10 being the safest. Our machine learning model continuously updates these scores based on real-time data and historical trends.
Fastest Route: Prioritizes time efficiency with minimal travel duration.
Safest Route: Prioritizes safety factors even if it takes longer.
Balanced Route: Optimal combination of safety and speed for most situations.
Currently, SafeRoute Navigator requires an internet connection for real-time safety data and route calculations. However, we're working on offline functionality for emergency situations and areas with poor connectivity.
You can report issues directly through the app using the "Report Issue" button on any route, or contact our support team. We take all reports seriously and use them to improve our algorithms and data accuracy.
